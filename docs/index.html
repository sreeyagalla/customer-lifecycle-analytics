Customer Lifecycle Scorecard

How are we doing at acquiring, activating, retaining and winning back customers versus the same quarter last year, and do our customer programs actually move behaviour? Every number on this page is computed with SQL (DuckDB) and Python from two real public retail datasets, and is significance-tested before it is called a change.

Honest scope

  • All data is real; nothing is synthetic. Retail: UCI Online Retail II (CC BY 4.0), a UK online gift-ware retailer whose customers are largely wholesalers, Dec 2009 to Dec 2011. Programs: the MineThatData e-mail experiment (Hillstrom, 2008), a different, apparel retailer. The two datasets are never joined.
  • Retail customers are identified on only of staged lines; lines without a customer ID are excluded from customer KPIs.
  • Significance tests treat each quarter's customers as independent samples. Some customers appear in both years, which makes the rate tests conservative.
  • Email lift is causal (randomized). Retail diagnostics are correlations, not causes.
  • Scorecard badges test each KPI on its own and are not corrected for browsing many segment × quarter combinations, so expect the occasional false alarm. The hypothesis registry is corrected (Benjamini-Hochberg).
  • No forecasts, targets or business-impact figures are invented. "Incremental" totals are measured inside the experiment population only.
